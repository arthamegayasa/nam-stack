# Profile: idea

For research topics, study ideas, product ideas, and plans.

- **Artifact**: an idea brief in Markdown: question, background, novelty, approach, feasibility, risks, and ethics. If the user gave only loose text, write the brief from it first; that brief is `r0`.
- **Roles**: grant reviewer (significance, novelty, feasibility) and domain expert (clinical or field realism). Add a skeptic for high-stakes ideas.
- **Evidence**: a literature search for prior work and novelty (PubMed and web, through the research skills), plus feasibility facts with sources: data access, sample size, cost, timeline.
- **Rubric starters**: significance, novelty, feasibility, clarity of the question, ethics and risk.
- **Invariants by default**: the core question and population. A pivot goes to the user as ESCALATE. Every claim about prior work carries a citation.
- **Helpers**: `research`, `academic-research-suite` (literature review), and a deep-research skill when available.
- **Versioning**: `<name>.r<n>.md`.
- **Change budget default**: at most two sections of the brief rewritten per round.
